# PyTodo Pro · Residual risk assessment

The ratings assume a conventional multi-user release where separate accounts expect task isolation. Likelihood describes exposure to the failure under intended use or plausible deployment conditions. It is a QA judgment, with no estimated probabilities. Open defects retain their Week 7 severities and priorities independently of risk scores. [Week 7 strategy](../Week%207/Test%20Strategy.md) · [Defect register](MASTER_DEFECT_REGISTER.csv)

- **Likelihood** 3 means reproducible on a normal or readily encountered path, 2 means evidence supports a conditional trigger or a plausible gap on an intended path, and 1 means context-specific or weak evidence about exposure.
- **Impact** 3 means potential cross-account compromise, persistent data loss/corruption, service disruption or exclusion from core workflows. 2 means material errors, impairment or degraded accountability. 1 means limited friction or presentation impact.
- **Risk level** is likelihood × impact. Scores of 9 are Critical, 6 High, 3–4 Medium and 1–2 Low. Within a score, prioritize higher impact, then use risk ID as a stable tie-breaker.
