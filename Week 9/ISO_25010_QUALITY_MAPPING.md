# PyTodo Pro · ISO/IEC 25010 quality mapping

## Edition and scope

The supplied course brief names ISO/IEC 25010 in its Week 9 section without a publication year, diagram or characteristic list. The repository has no lecture slides or edition-specific handout. **The edition used in class is unable to be verified.** This assessment adopts **ISO/IEC 25010:2023, edition 2**, as its explicit product-quality reference. ISO identifies the 2011 edition as withdrawn and describes its product model as having eight characteristics. The 2023 product model has nine. If class materials use the 2011 model, use the terminology crosswalk below and confirm the edition with the instructor. [Project brief, p. 6](../Group%20Project.pdf) · [ISO/IEC 25010:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78176.html) · [ISO/IEC 25010:2011](https://www.iso.org/standard/35733.html/)

The classifications describe depth of evidence within the course scope. **Directly Evaluated** means a dedicated campaign tested the central in-scope property against explicit expected results. **Partially Evaluated** means some subcharacteristics were targeted but significant parts of the intended context or characteristic remain open. **Indirectly Evaluated** means the evidence comes from adjacent observations or engineering proxies. **Not Evaluated** means there is no relevant assessment evidence. These are evidence classifications. A Directly Evaluated characteristic can have failed results. The mapping carries no certification or full-compliance claim.

## Terminology crosswalk and priorities

For a 2011 product-model presentation, keep the evidence and classifications for Functional suitability, Performance efficiency, Compatibility, Reliability, Security and Maintainability. Use **Usability** for the Interaction capability row, associating observable keyboard and accessibility evidence with the 2011 operability and accessibility subcharacteristics. Use **Portability** for the Flexibility row, limiting the mapping to adaptability, installability and replaceability. The 2011 product model has no standalone Safety row. The 2023 additions and terminology changes require care when interpreting the comparison. [ISO first-edition product model preview](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso-iec%3A25010%3Aed-1%3Av1%3Aen) · [ISO product-quality measurement draft terminology](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso%3Astd%3Aiso-iec%3A25023%3Adis%3Aed-1%3Av1%3Aen)

Prioritise owner isolation and session controls, failed-write atomicity and recovery, inclusive operation on native platforms, and representative capacity verification.
